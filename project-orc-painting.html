Orc Warrior | Painting

Visual Development • Concept Illustration

The Project

This digital painting focuses on the fierce, battle-hardened aesthetic of an original Orc, heavily inspired by the iconic warriors of the World of Warcraft universe. Every detail—from the raw power of the anatomy to the weathered textures of the armor—was designed as a technical blueprint for the digital sculpture featured on this site. This comprehensive 2D-to-3D workflow was developed specifically for the creation of a 1/6 scale premium collectible figure.
